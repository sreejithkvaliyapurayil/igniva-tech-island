What you can review at a project briefing

  • Data centre anchor tenants: capacity, technical requirements and handover needs.
  • Enterprise and coworking teams: team size, workspace requirements and occupancy plans.
  • Institutional and strategic partners: project milestones, potential roles and due diligence materials. For a proposed LLP partnership, ask which legal entity would receive your contribution and request the current LLP record, beneficiary details and draft agreement.

IGNIVA Tech Island is a proposed development on Willingdon Island, Kochi. Phase I proposes AI IT parks, enterprise workspace and a play area. Phase II proposes a dedicated data centre.

Before making a contribution, request the registered lease, permitted-use documents and applicable consents. Review how the agreement defines profit and loss sharing, voting, transfers and exit rights. Ask for the financial information supporting any distribution or exit scenario, including any signed funding commitments the scenario relies on. Illustrative projections describe possible outcomes. Distributions depend on project performance, available cash and the signed terms. Monthly payouts and return of capital at 36 months are not guaranteed.
