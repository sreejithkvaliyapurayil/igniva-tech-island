Phase II · Proposed development · Willingdon Island, Kochi

Kochi Data Centre

A proposed data centre for GPU and other high-density workloads, planned as a powered-and-cooled shell on a separate, secure five-acre parcel. A prospective anchor tenant would install and operate its own IT equipment.
