Our Leadership

Meet the founder and Chairman & Managing Director of IGNIVA Group.

Nijesh Puthalath

Chairman & Managing Director

Nijesh Puthalath founded IGNIVA Group in 2015. His work centres on developing commercial assets and destinations for business, trade, hospitality and entertainment.

He holds an MBA from London and brings experience in corporate banking, financial services, business development and investments. He is active in the startup ecosystems of India and the UAE, with interests in strategic partnerships, early-stage funding and brand expansion.

Leadership approach

His executive profile emphasises long-term asset creation, strategic investment, infrastructure development and commercial partnerships. Under his leadership, the group expanded across convention and trade centres, hospitality, logistics, retail and technology-led developments.
