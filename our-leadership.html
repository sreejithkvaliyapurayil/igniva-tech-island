The founder’s story

From finance to infrastructure development

Nijesh Puthalath earned an MBA in London and worked across corporate banking, financial services, business development and investments. In 2015, he founded IGNIVA Group in Kerala to develop long-term commercial assets.

Convention and trade centres became the group’s flagship business. Hospitality, logistics and retail expanded the portfolio to serve visitors, tenants and businesses in different ways.

His work in the startup ecosystems of India and the UAE includes an interest in early-stage funding, strategic partnerships and brand expansion. His leadership approach centres on long-term asset creation and building commercial partnerships around each development.
