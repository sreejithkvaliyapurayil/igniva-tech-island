Nijesh Puthalath established IGNIVA Group in 2015. His work focuses on developing commercial assets and integrated destinations for business, commerce, entertainment and everyday life.

Based in Kerala, the group spans convention and trade centres, hospitality, logistics, commercial destinations and technology retail. His approach combines long-term asset creation with strategic investment, commercial partnerships and professional management.

An MBA graduate from London, he brings experience in corporate banking, financial services, business development and investments. He is active in the startup ecosystems of India and the UAE, with interests in strategic partnerships, early-stage funding, hospitality and brand expansion.

Our journey

From Kerala to a new chapter in Kochi

Under Nijesh Puthalath’s leadership, IGNIVA has grown from a Kerala-based enterprise into a group spanning trade, hospitality, retail, logistics and proposed technology infrastructure.

2015 · Founding Trade and events A broader portfolio The next chapter

  1. 2015 · Kerala

    IGNIVA Group begins

    Drawing on experience in corporate banking, financial services and investment, Nijesh Puthalath founded IGNIVA Group to develop commercial assets for the long term. The approach combined investment, infrastructure development and partnerships with the businesses that would use those spaces.

  2. Convention and trade

    Creating room for business to gather

    Convention and exhibition venues became a central part of the group’s portfolio. Calicut Trade Centre and Cochin Trade Centre bring the group’s work in trade, exhibitions and live events to two Kerala cities.

    Explore the trade centres ↗

  3. Hospitality, retail and logistics

    Expanding into new business sectors

    IGNIVA Airport Hotel, Calicut Beach Bay and 4in Bazar Tech Mall extended the portfolio into hospitality and retail. IGNIVA Logistics Hub added warehousing and distribution space for commercial tenants.

    Explore the portfolio ↗

  4. Proposed development · Willingdon Island

    Looking ahead to Tech Island

    Phase I proposes AI IT parks, coworking and a play area. Phase II proposes a dedicated data centre. Together, the two phases extend IGNIVA’s work into technology and digital infrastructure.

    Explore IGNIVA Tech Island ↗
