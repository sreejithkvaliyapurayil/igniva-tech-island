The Work Club

Space for the way your team works

A proposed workspace for founders, independent professionals, creators and enterprise teams, with flexible desks, private offices, team suites, shared meeting and event rooms, and collaboration lounges.

Team space and enterprise services are planned alongside cafés, wellness and leisure amenities. The exact workspace and amenity mix will be confirmed during design and operator review.

Tenant experience

Workspace and visitor services

Workspace for AI and IT Park tenants

Flexible offices and team suites envisioned for AI startups and enterprise technology teams.

AI-powered robotic reception

A proposed robot concierge could welcome visitors and help them find their way around the campus.

ISLE Play

Fitness, recreation and creative space

The proposed district combines outdoor training, family recreation, culture and places to meet. A social street with cafés and outdoor seating gives teams and visitors space to gather.

Train outdoors

A training yard and shaded open gym for functional and group sessions.

Play together

Electric karting, gaming and simulators, and family recreation for casual visits and group outings.

Make something

Themed micro-studios for shoots, public art and spaces for creative gatherings and cultural programming.

Images are illustrative. Specific attractions remain subject to design, feasibility and approvals.
